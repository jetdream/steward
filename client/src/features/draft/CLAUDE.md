# features/draft/ — the draft, opened (XH-13)

**Purpose.** A Ready card expanded for deep review, rendered as the body of the
`DSS-24` summoned pane. Realizes `APRS-1 v3` (per-channel variants, fit,
dispositions) and `APRS-3` (raw inline edit, diffed).

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `draft.ts` | the deterministic rules — default variant, plain-language times, schedule rows. Unit-tested |
| `DraftOpened.tsx` | the pane body. The pane itself belongs to `SummonedSurface` |

Pixels: frame 2 of `design/mockups/exp-38-home/round-1/Steward Home (desktop).html`.

## Gotchas

- **A skipped channel KEEPS its tab and its row.** GENS-5 retains the variant
  with its reason so the omission can be seen and overridden. Filtering it out
  would let a founder approve believing a post goes somewhere it does not —
  on the one screen built for looking closely.
- **Anything reading the summon context must be a COMPONENT rendered inside the
  home.** `useReady` is a hook called by the screen ABOVE the shell, so a
  `useSummon()` there resolves outside the provider and silently becomes the
  no-op — button renders, click registers, nothing opens (LRN-33).
- **The pane closes through the context**, never by finding the back button in
  the DOM. A selector for another component's control breaks silently the day it
  is renamed.

## Not yet, stated rather than faked

- **Adjust (guided)** — CHTS-5's guided redraft has no procedure behind it. The
  verb says what it is waiting for; Edit (raw) and Redirect are live.
- **Undo after approve** — APRS-1's recall (approved → draft while still
  pending) has no procedure either, so the pane offers no undo it cannot honour.
  Carry this into `MANUAL-EVAL.md`'s known limitations.
- The NEWS long-form variant, topic tags and ArticleLink badges ride with
  `@news`, out of this phase's scope.
